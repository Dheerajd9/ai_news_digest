### 🎓 This week's lesson: Off-grid microgrids
An **off-grid microgrid** is a self-contained energy system that generates power locally on-site without relying on the public power grid. When standard commercial facilities require electricity, they connect to regional utility power lines. However, modern AI data center clusters consume massive gigawatts of electricity, creating 5-to-7-year waiting lists for grid connections. To bypass public infrastructure delays, technology companies build private microgrids powered by gas turbines, solar arrays, or batteries. This secures continuous power for servers but isolates corporate infrastructure from community utility systems.
