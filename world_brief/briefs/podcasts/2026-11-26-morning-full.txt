EMMA: A Variable Rate Repo, or VRR, is a monetary tool used by central banks like the RBI to inject or absorb short-term liquidity from the banking system. Unlike fixed-rate auctions where the interest rate is predetermined, banks bid competitively for funds based on market demand.
